# Subscription Plans

Cursor has individual plans for one person, plus Teams and Enterprise plans for organizations. Current individual plans are Free, Plus, Premium, Super and Ultra, with more included usage at each step. Cloud Agents, Automations, Bugbot, Auto and third-party models need Premium or higher, and older plans such as Pro and Pro+ keep working until you change them.
