# Third-Party Models

Third-party models are models from providers such as Anthropic, OpenAI and Google that you can pick in Cursor next to its own models. They are not part of the weekly pool. On individual plans they need Premium or higher and are billed at the provider's API list price from prepaid credit, and on Teams and Enterprise a Cursor Token Rate applies to eligible models.
