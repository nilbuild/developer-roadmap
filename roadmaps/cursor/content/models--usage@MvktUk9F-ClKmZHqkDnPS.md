# Models & Usage

Models and usage cover which model runs your task and what it costs. You can pick a model, set its reasoning effort, let Auto choose for you, and use Cursor's own models or third-party ones. Paid plans include a pool of usage, and when it runs out you can wait for the reset, add credit or upgrade.

Visit the following resources to learn more:

- [@official@Models & Pricing](https://cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing)
- [@official@Usage limits](https://cursor.com/help/models-and-usage/usage-limits)
