# Side Chats

A side chat is a full agent conversation linked to your main chat. It gets the main conversation as hidden reference context, but its own questions and answers stay out of the main thread. Open one with `/side` or `/btw`, or select text or a diff and choose "Ask in Side Chat". You can @-mention the side chat in the main thread to bring its findings back.

Visit the following resources to learn more:

- [@official@Side chats](https://cursor.com/help/ai-features/side-chats)
