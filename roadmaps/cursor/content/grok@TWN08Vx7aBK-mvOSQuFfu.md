# Grok

Grok is a model family that Cursor offers as one of its own models, with Grok 4.7, 4.6 and 4.5 available. Each has Fast variants that respond quicker at a higher price. Because they are Cursor models, they draw from your plan's weekly pool instead of prepaid credit.

Visit the following resources to learn more:

- [@official@Grok 4.7](https://cursor.com/docs/models/grok-4-7)
- [@official@Models & Pricing](https://cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing)
