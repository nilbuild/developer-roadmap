# Choosing Models

The model picker in the agent panel lets you choose between Cursor's own models and models from providers such as Anthropic, OpenAI and Google. In the CLI, use `/model`, `--model` or `agent models` to see what is available. Pick a faster model for simple edits and a stronger one for hard reasoning.
