# Cursor Models

Cursor Models are the models Cursor offers itself: Composer 2.5 and the Grok 4.7, 4.6 and 4.5 family. They draw from the weekly pool included with paid plans, and the Plus plan can use only these models. Third-party models from other providers are billed separately.

Visit the following resources to learn more:

- [@official@Models & Pricing](https://cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing)
- [@official@Available models](https://cursor.com/help/models-and-usage/available-models)
