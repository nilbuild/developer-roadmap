# Resuming

Cursor keeps your past chats so you can return to them. In the CLI, `agent ls` lists previous chats, `agent resume` or `--continue` opens the latest one and `--resume <id>` loads a specific thread. Inside a session, `/resume` opens a list of recent chats. In the Agents Window, `Cmd+K` opens a palette that searches your past transcripts, and `Cmd+F` finds text inside an open conversation.
