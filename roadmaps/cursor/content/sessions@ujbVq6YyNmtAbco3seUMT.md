# Sessions

A session is a saved chat with the agent, including its context and history. Cursor keeps your past chats so you can come back to them. You can resume one, fork it into a new chat, rename it, rewind to an earlier message, and summarize a long conversation to free context.

Visit the following resources to learn more:
