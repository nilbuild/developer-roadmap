# Monitoring and Controlling Risks

Monitoring and controlling risks involves tracking identified risks over time and watching for new ones as a product develops. It requires regular review of the risk register, updated assessments, and confirmation that mitigation actions are working. This is an ongoing activity throughout the product lifecycle, not a one-time exercise.
