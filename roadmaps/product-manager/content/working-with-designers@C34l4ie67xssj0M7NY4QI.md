# Working with Designers

A PM and a designer share ownership of the product experience. The PM brings the problem, the users, and the business goals, and the designer brings the solutions to how it should look and work. Good collaboration means giving designers context instead of ready-made solutions, involving them early in discovery, and giving feedback about the problem, not about colors and layouts. Disagreements are settled with user evidence where possible.
