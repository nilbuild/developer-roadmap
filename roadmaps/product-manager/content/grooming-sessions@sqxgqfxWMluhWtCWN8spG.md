# Grooming Sessions

Grooming sessions, also called backlog refinement, are regular meetings where the team reviews, clarifies, and estimates upcoming work items. The goal is to ensure that the top of the backlog is ready for sprint planning. Effective grooming keeps the team moving without constant interruption for clarification during development.
