# Idea Generation

Idea generation is the process of finding problems and opportunities worth building a product around, and then collecting possible solutions. It involves looking at market gaps, user pain points, and business potential, and then filtering weak ideas early. Techniques such as brainstorming help produce options, and later research and testing decide which ones go forward.
