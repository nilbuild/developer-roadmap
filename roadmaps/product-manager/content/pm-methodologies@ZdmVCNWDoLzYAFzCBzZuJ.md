# PM Methodologies

Product methodologies are the working styles a team uses to plan, build, and ship. Options range from Scrum sprints to continuous flow with Kanban, and each fits different team sizes and kinds of work. A PM does not need to follow one strictly, but needs to understand what each one offers so the team can choose a process that fits its situation.
