# MAU (Monthly Active Users)

Monthly Active Users (MAU) is the number of unique users who meaningfully engage with a product within a 30-day period. What counts as "active" should be defined by the product team, for example logging in, completing a core action, or making a purchase, and the definition should stay consistent so the metric can be compared over time. MAU is used to track growth and the size of the engaged user base, and it is often compared with Daily Active Users (DAU): the DAU/MAU ratio, sometimes called stickiness, shows how often monthly users come back each day.
