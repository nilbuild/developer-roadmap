# Agile & Scrum

Agile is a set of values for building software in small steps and adjusting based on feedback. Scrum is the most common Agile framework. It organizes work into fixed-length sprints with a prioritized backlog and regular events: planning, daily standup, review, and retrospective. Most PM job descriptions assume you know this shared vocabulary, since the PM usually works closely with the Product Owner role.
