# Slack

Slack is a messaging platform organized around channels, direct messages, and integrations with other tools. Product teams use it for day-to-day coordination, sharing updates, and reducing email volume. Good Slack hygiene, including clear channel naming and notification norms, keeps communication manageable as teams grow.
