# Risk Identification Techniques

Risk identification techniques are structured methods for uncovering potential threats to a product's success. Common approaches include brainstorming sessions, assumption mapping, pre-mortems, and checklists drawn from past projects. Using multiple techniques increases the likelihood of catching risks that a single approach would miss.
