# Principles of UX Design

UX design principles are guidelines that inform how to design interfaces that are usable, efficient, and satisfying. Common principles include visibility of system status, consistency, error prevention, and user control. They are derived from research in cognitive psychology and years of empirical observation in human-computer interaction.
