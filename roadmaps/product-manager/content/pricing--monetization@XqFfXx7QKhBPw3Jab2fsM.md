# Pricing & Monetization

Pricing is the decision of how much to charge and in which form, such as per seat, per use, or in tiered plans. Monetization covers the packaging of features into plans and the tests used to learn what customers will pay. AI products make this harder because every request has a real cost, so many companies are moving from per-seat to usage-based pricing. PMs usually work with finance and marketing on it.
