# Accessibility

Accessibility is designing products so people with disabilities can use them, including people who are blind, deaf, color blind, or unable to use a mouse. Common practices include enough color contrast, text alternatives for images, keyboard navigation, and captions on video. The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) are the standard reference. Many countries also have laws that require it, so it is a legal and business concern as well as a design one.
