# Opportunity Solution Trees

An opportunity solution tree is a diagram that links a business outcome to the customer needs that could move it, the solutions for each need, and the experiments that test each solution. The top of the tree is one outcome, such as higher retention. Every idea must connect to a customer opportunity above it. The structure pushes teams to compare several solutions instead of building the first idea they like.
