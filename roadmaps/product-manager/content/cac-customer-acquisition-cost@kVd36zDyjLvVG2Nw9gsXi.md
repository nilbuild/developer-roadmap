# CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)

Customer Acquisition Cost is the total cost of acquiring a new customer, including marketing and sales expenses divided by the number of customers acquired. A healthy business maintains an LTV-to-CAC ratio above 3:1. Rising CAC without a corresponding increase in LTV signals declining efficiency in growth.
