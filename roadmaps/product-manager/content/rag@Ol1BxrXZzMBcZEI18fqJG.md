# RAG

RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) is a technique where a system first searches your own documents for relevant information and then gives it to the model along with the question. The model answers using that material instead of relying only on its training. This makes answers more current and specific, and it can show where the answer came from. Quality depends on how well the search step finds the right content.
