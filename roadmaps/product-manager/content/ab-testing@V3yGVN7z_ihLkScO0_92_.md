# A/B Testing in Data-Driven Decision Making

A/B testing is a controlled experiment where two variants of a product element are shown to separate user groups to determine which performs better. The variant with stronger results on the target metric is adopted. It is widely used in conversion optimization, onboarding design, and feature development.
