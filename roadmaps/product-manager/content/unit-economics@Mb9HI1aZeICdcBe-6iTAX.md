# Unit Economics

Unit economics measure whether a business makes money on a single customer. They compare what it costs to acquire and serve one customer against the revenue that customer brings over time. Key figures include gross margin, payback period, and the ratio of lifetime value to acquisition cost. If the numbers per customer do not work, growth only increases the losses.
