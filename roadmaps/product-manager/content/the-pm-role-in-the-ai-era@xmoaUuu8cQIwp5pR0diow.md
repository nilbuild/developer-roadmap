# The PM Role in the AI Era

AI tools now handle a large part of the routine work in product management, such as drafting documents, summarizing research, and building quick prototypes. PMs spend less time producing artifacts and more time on judgment: choosing what to build, deciding what good looks like, and checking what AI produces. The role also splits into two kinds of work, using AI to do the job faster and managing products that have AI inside them.
