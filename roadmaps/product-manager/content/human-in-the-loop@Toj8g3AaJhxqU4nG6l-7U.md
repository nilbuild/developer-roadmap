# Human-in-the-Loop

Human-in-the-loop means a person reviews or approves what an AI system does before it has an effect. The level of review depends on the risk: a suggested email subject needs none, while a refund or a medical note needs a check. Designing it well means making review quick and showing the person what they need to decide. Too many approvals make the feature slow and people stop reading them.
