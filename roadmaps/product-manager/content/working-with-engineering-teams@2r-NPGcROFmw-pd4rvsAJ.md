# Working with Engineering Teams

Working with engineering teams requires product managers to translate user needs and business goals into clear, prioritized requirements that engineers can act on. It also involves unblocking dependencies, managing scope, and maintaining trust through consistent communication. A strong PM-engineering relationship produces better outcomes than one where either side feels ignored.
