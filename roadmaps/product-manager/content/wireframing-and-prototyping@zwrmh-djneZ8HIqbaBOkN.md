# Wireframing and Prototyping – UX / UI Design

Wireframing is the process of creating low-fidelity sketches or diagrams that show the layout and structure of a screen. Prototyping takes this further by building interactive mockups that simulate user flows. Both practices allow teams to validate ideas cheaply before committing to full development.
