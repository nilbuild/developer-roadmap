# Leadership and Influence in Product Management

Leadership and influence in product management refer to the ability to guide teams and stakeholders toward a shared outcome without relying on formal authority. Product managers lead through vision, credibility, and relationship building rather than direct control. Strong leadership makes teams more aligned, motivated, and effective.
