# Privacy & Compliance

Privacy and compliance cover the laws and rules about how a product collects, stores, and uses data. Examples are GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California, and industry rules such as HIPAA for health data. A PM helps decide what data to collect, how to ask for consent, and how long to keep it. AI features add questions, such as whether customer data is used to train models.
