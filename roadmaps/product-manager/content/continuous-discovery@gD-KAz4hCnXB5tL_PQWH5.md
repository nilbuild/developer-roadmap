# Continuous Discovery

Continuous discovery is the habit of talking to customers every week instead of running research once before a project. The product trio of PM, designer, and engineer usually does short interviews together and uses what they learn to make decisions in the same week. The approach comes from Teresa Torres and her book Continuous Discovery Habits. It replaces the old pattern of one big research phase followed by months without user contact.
