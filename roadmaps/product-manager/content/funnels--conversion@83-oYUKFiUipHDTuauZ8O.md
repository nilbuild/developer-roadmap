# Funnels & Conversion

A funnel is the sequence of steps a user takes toward a goal, such as visiting a page, signing up, and paying. Conversion rate is the percentage of users who complete the desired action, either at one step or from start to end. Looking at each step shows where people drop off and where a change would have the most effect.
