# Identifying Stakeholders

Identifying stakeholders means mapping out all individuals and groups who affect or are affected by the product. This includes internal stakeholders like executives, engineers, and sales teams, as well as external ones like customers and partners. A thorough stakeholder map is the starting point for effective engagement planning.
