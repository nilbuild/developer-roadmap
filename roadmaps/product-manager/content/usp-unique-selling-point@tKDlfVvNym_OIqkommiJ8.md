# USP (Unique Selling Point)
 
A unique selling point is the specific quality that makes a product distinctly better or different from alternatives for a defined audience. It should be something real and defensible, not just a marketing claim. A clear USP simplifies messaging and helps users understand why they should choose one product over another.
