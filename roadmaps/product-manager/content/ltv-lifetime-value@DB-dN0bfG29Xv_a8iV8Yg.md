# Understanding LTV (Lifetime Value) as a Product Manager

Lifetime Value estimates the total revenue a business can expect from a single customer over the duration of their relationship with the product. It is used to evaluate how much a company can sustainably spend on acquiring new customers. LTV is most meaningful when compared to CAC.
