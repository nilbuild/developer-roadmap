# AI Fundamentals

AI fundamentals are the minimum technical knowledge a PM needs to work on products that use AI models. This includes how large language models produce text, what they can and cannot do reliably, and how companies add their own data to them. A PM does not need to train models. The goal is to judge what is feasible, ask engineers good questions, and set realistic expectations.
