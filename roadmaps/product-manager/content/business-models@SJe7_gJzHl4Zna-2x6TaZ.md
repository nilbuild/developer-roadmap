# Business Models

A business model describes how a product creates value for customers and earns money from it. Common models are subscription, usage-based, freemium, marketplace, advertising, and licensing. The choice affects what the product team builds, how it measures success, and who it competes with. A PM does not need to design the whole model alone, but needs to understand it well enough to make product decisions that fit it.
