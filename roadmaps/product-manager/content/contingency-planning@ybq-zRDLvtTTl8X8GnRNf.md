# Contingency Planning in Risk Mitigation

Contingency planning involves preparing specific responses for risks that are likely or high-impact enough to warrant a predetermined plan. A contingency plan defines what the team will do if a risk occurs, reducing response time and decision-making pressure during a crisis. Not every risk requires a contingency, only the ones with serious consequences.
