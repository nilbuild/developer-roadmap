# Building and Leading Teams

Building and leading teams involves assembling the right people, establishing shared norms, and creating conditions where the team can do its best work. It includes fostering psychological safety, setting clear expectations, and managing performance over time. Product managers who lead well create teams that are resilient and self-improving.
