# What is Product Management?

Product management is the practice of overseeing a product's strategy, development, and performance throughout its lifecycle. Product managers define the vision, prioritize the roadmap, and coordinate across engineering, design, marketing, and sales to bring products to market. The goal is to build things users want that also serve business objectives.
