# Usability Testing

Usability testing is watching real users try to complete specific tasks in a product or prototype. The team notes where people hesitate, make errors, or give up. The findings improve navigation, labels, and interactions. Tests can be moderated by a researcher or run remotely and unmoderated, and even five participants usually reveal the main problems.
