# Prioritization Techniques

Prioritization techniques are structured methods for ranking product work by impact, effort, confidence, or strategic fit. Common frameworks include RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort), MoSCoW (Must Have, Should Have, Could Have, Won't Have), and the Kano model. Using a technique consistently makes prioritization decisions easier to explain and defend.
