# Predictive Analytics

Predictive analytics applies machine learning and statistical models to forecast future user behavior, product performance, or market trends based on historical data. In product management, it enables proactive decisions about churn prevention, feature investment, and capacity planning. It requires clean data pipelines and domain knowledge to interpret responsibly.
