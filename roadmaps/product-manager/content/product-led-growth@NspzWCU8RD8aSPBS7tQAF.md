# Product-Led Growth

Product-led growth (PLG) is a strategy where the product itself drives sign-ups, adoption, and upgrades, instead of relying mainly on sales or marketing. Free trials, freemium plans, and easy self-serve onboarding are typical tools. Users experience the value first and pay later, often with sales joining only for larger accounts. It needs a product that is easy to start and a quick path to activation.
