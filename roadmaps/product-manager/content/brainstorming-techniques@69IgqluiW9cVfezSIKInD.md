# Brainstorming Techniques for Product Identification

Brainstorming techniques are structured methods for generating a large number of ideas quickly in a group or individual setting. Common approaches include mind mapping, free association, round-robin ideation, and reverse brainstorming. The goal is to defer judgment and expand the option space before narrowing down.
