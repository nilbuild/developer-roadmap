# Trust & Verification

AI output can look confident and still be wrong, with invented facts, wrong numbers, or made-up sources. Verification means checking claims against the original source before using them, especially anything you share with others. Trust also covers data safety: knowing which customer or company information you must not paste into a tool, and following your company's policy. A good habit is to ask for sources and then open them.
