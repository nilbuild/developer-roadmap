# Aha as a Roadmapping Tool

Aha is a product roadmapping and strategy tool that supports goal setting, initiative tracking, and visual roadmap creation. It integrates with project management and development tools to keep strategy and execution connected. It is popular in enterprise environments where multiple products need coordinated planning.
