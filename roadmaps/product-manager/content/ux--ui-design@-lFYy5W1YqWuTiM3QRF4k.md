# UX / UI Design
 
UX/UI design covers the practice of designing how a product looks and how users interact with it. UX (user experience) focuses on the structure, flow, and usability of an interface, while UI (user interface) focuses on the visual layer. Together they determine whether a product is intuitive and pleasant to use.
