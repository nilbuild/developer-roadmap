# AI Moats

A moat is an advantage that keeps competitors from copying a product easily. Common moats are network effects, switching costs, scale, brand, and proprietary data. When anyone can build a feature quickly with AI and most companies use the same models, features alone are easy to copy. Proprietary data, distribution, and deep integration into a customer's daily workflow become the stronger sources of defense.
