# Failure Modes

Failure modes are the specific ways an AI feature can go wrong. Examples are wrong answers, refusing a valid request, ignoring instructions, producing harmful content, leaking private data, and slowing down or timing out. Listing them before launch lets the team design fallbacks, warnings, and easy ways for users to correct or report problems. Real user logs are the best source for finding new ones after release.
