# Risk Management

Risk management is spotting what could go wrong with a product and deciding what to do about it before it happens. Common product risks are unvalidated user needs, technical limits, unclear requirements, and dependencies on other teams. A PM lists the risks, judges how likely and how serious each one is, and plans a response, such as testing the riskiest assumption first. The practice is lightweight: a short list that the team reviews regularly works better than a heavy process.
