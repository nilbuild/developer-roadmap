# Evals

Evals are structured tests that measure whether an AI feature gives good enough results. A team collects example inputs, defines what a good answer is, and scores the output automatically or with human review. Running evals after every change shows if a new model or prompt made things better or worse. Many practitioners treat writing evals as the central skill of AI product management.
