# Phased Rollouts

A phased rollout is a release strategy where a new feature is made available to a small percentage of users first, then gradually expanded to the full user base. This approach reduces risk by limiting exposure while the team monitors for bugs or unexpected behavior. Phases are typically defined by geography, user segment, or random percentage.
