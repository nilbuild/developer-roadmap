# Notion as Roadmapping Tool

Notion is a flexible workspace tool that combines documents, databases, and wikis in a single interface. Product teams use it for roadmaps, PRDs, meeting notes, and knowledge bases. Its flexibility makes it popular for smaller teams that want to consolidate their documentation in one place.
