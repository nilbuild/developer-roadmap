# Cost & Latency

Cost and latency describe how much an AI request costs and how long it takes to answer. Models charge by the amount of text processed, so long inputs, long answers, and agents with many steps cost more. Slow responses hurt the experience, and larger models are usually slower. PMs use these numbers to set pricing, choose models, and decide where AI is worth its price.
