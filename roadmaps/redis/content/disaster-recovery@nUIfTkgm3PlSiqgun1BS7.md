# Disaster Recovery

Disaster recovery for Redis involves having off-site backups of RDB or AOF files and a documented process for restoring them. Replication across availability zones or data centers reduces the risk of losing data in a regional failure. Redis Sentinel and Cluster both support configurations that span multiple failure domains.
