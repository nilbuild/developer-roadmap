# Sort and Sweep

Sort and sweep, also called sweep and prune, is a broad-phase collision detection technique. Objects' bounding volumes are projected onto one or more axes, and the projections are sorted. Pairs whose projections overlap on all axes are flagged as potential collision candidates for narrow-phase testing.
