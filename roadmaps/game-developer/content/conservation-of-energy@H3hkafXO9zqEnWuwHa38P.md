# Conservation of Energy

Conservation of energy in rendering means that a surface cannot reflect more light than it receives. PBR shading models enforce this principle by ensuring that the sum of diffuse and specular contributions does not exceed the incoming irradiance. Violating energy conservation produces unnaturally bright surfaces that break physical plausibility.
