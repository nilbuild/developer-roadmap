# Hermite

A Hermite curve is a cubic polynomial segment defined by its two endpoints and the tangent vectors at each endpoint. The tangent vectors control the direction and speed at which the curve leaves and arrives at each point. Hermite curves are used in animation systems where controlling tangents gives artists precise influence over motion easing.
