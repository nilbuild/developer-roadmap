# Sampling

Sampling in graphics refers to how discrete values are taken from continuous signals such as textures or light distributions. Aliasing artifacts appear when a scene contains detail at frequencies higher than the sampling rate, which is why techniques like multisampling, supersampling, and temporal anti-aliasing are used. Importance sampling is also central to Monte Carlo rendering methods.
