# Microsurface Scattering

Microsurface scattering describes how light interacts with the microscopic irregularities of a surface, which are too small to model with geometry but affect the overall reflectance. PBR materials use microfacet models like GGX or Beckmann to represent the statistical distribution of these tiny surface normals. The roughness parameter controls how broad or sharp the resulting specular highlight appears.
