# Convexity

Convexity describes the property of a shape where any line segment connecting two points inside the shape remains entirely inside it. Convex shapes are simpler to process in physics simulations because algorithms like GJK can solve intersection and distance queries efficiently for them. Non-convex meshes are usually decomposed into convex parts for simulation.
