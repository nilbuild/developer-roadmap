# Projection

Projection transforms 3D coordinates into 2D screen coordinates, discarding or encoding depth information. The two main types used in games are perspective projection, which produces the foreshortening effect of depth, and orthographic projection, which preserves parallel lines without depth distortion. Projection matrices encode these transformations and are applied as the final step in the vertex pipeline.
