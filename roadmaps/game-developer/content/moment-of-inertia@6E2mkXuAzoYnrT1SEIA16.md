# Moment of Inertia

The moment of inertia is the rotational analog of mass, describing how resistant an object is to changes in its rotational motion. It depends on both the object's mass and how that mass is distributed relative to the axis of rotation. In physics engines, the moment of inertia is represented as a 3x3 inertia tensor that transforms applied torques into angular accelerations.
