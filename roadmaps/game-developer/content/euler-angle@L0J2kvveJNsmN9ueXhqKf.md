# Euler Angle

Euler angles represent a 3D rotation as three sequential rotations around the coordinate axes, typically labeled pitch, yaw, and roll. They are intuitive for humans to read and author but suffer from gimbal lock, where two rotation axes align and a degree of freedom is lost. Euler angles are often used in editor tools and exported from animation software, then converted to quaternions at runtime.
