# Force

A force is a vector quantity that causes a change in an object's momentum when applied to it. In rigid body simulation, forces are accumulated each frame and integrated to produce acceleration and velocity changes. Different force types include gravity, spring forces, drag, and impulses applied instantaneously during collisions.
