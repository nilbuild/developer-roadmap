# Center of Mass

The center of mass is the average position of all the mass in an object, weighted by each part's mass. In rigid body simulation, forces applied at the center of mass produce pure linear acceleration with no rotation, while forces applied off-center also generate torque. Physics engines compute the center of mass from the collider geometry and mass distribution.
