# Visibility and Occlusion

Visibility and occlusion systems determine which parts of a scene are visible from the camera and which are hidden behind other geometry. These systems are used to skip rendering work for geometry that cannot be seen, improving performance. Techniques include frustum culling, occlusion culling, and portal-based visibility.
