# Affine Transformation

An affine transformation combines linear transformations like rotation and scaling with translation, mapping points from one coordinate space to another while preserving parallelism and ratios of distances along lines. In games, affine transformations are represented using 4x4 matrices in homogeneous coordinates. They are applied to vertices and normals to move geometry between object, world, and camera spaces.
