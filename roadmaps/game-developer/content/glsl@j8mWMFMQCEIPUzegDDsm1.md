# GLSL

GLSL (OpenGL Shading Language) is the shader language for OpenGL and Vulkan (via SPIR-V compilation). It follows a C-like syntax and supports vertex, fragment, geometry, tessellation, and compute shader stages. GLSL shaders are compiled at runtime by the driver in OpenGL or compiled to SPIR-V ahead of time for Vulkan.
