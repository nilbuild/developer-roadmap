# Dynamics

Dynamics is the branch of physics that studies how forces cause objects to move and change velocity over time. In game physics, dynamics systems integrate Newton's laws to update rigid body positions, velocities, and angular states each simulation step. Accurate dynamics simulation requires handling forces, torques, constraints, and collision responses together.
