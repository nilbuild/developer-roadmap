# Intersection

Intersection tests determine whether two geometric shapes overlap in space. These tests are used throughout physics and rendering to answer questions like whether a ray hit a surface or whether two colliders are touching. Different shape pairs (sphere-sphere, box-triangle, capsule-mesh) require different algorithms with varying cost and precision tradeoffs.
