# Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity over time, produced by a net force acting on an object according to Newton's second law. In game physics, acceleration is computed from the sum of all forces applied to a body divided by its mass, then integrated to update velocity. Gravity is the most common constant acceleration applied to dynamic objects.
