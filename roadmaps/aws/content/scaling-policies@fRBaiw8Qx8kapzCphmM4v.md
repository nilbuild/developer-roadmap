# Scaling Policies

Scaling policies determine when and how an Auto Scaling group changes its capacity. Target tracking policies adjust instance count to keep a metric like CPU utilization at a target value. Step scaling and simple scaling policies respond to CloudWatch alarms with predefined capacity changes.
