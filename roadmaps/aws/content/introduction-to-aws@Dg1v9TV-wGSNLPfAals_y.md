# Introduction to AWS

AWS is organized into regions and availability zones around the world, each hosting a range of services. You access AWS through the console, CLI, or SDKs, and most services are billed based on actual usage. Getting familiar with the core concepts like accounts, IAM, and regions is the starting point for working with AWS.
