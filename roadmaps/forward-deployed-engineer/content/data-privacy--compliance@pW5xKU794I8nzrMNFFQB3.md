# Data Privacy & Compliance

Data privacy and compliance involves handling personal or sensitive data according to applicable laws and organizational policies. Relevant regulations include GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, and others depending on the industry and geography. For AI systems, this includes what data is sent to third-party model APIs, what is stored, and how long it is retained.
