# NoSQL Databases

NoSQL databases store data in formats other than traditional relational tables, such as documents, key-value pairs, graphs, or wide columns. Examples include MongoDB, Redis, Cassandra, and DynamoDB. They are often chosen for use cases that require high write throughput, flexible schemas, or horizontal scalability across distributed systems.
