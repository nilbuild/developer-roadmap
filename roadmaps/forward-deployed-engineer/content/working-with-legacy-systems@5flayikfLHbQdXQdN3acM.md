# Working with Legacy Systems

Legacy systems are the established, often undocumented infrastructure that most large organizations run their core operations on. They may be on-prem databases with no API, ERP systems that took years and millions of dollars to migrate to, or internal tools written a decade ago that no one fully understands anymore. The approach is to build around them: wrapping them with APIs, querying them safely, and connecting them to modern infrastructure without replacing the parts the business depends on.
