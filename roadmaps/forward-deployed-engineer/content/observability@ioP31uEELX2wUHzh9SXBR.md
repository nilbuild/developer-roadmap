# Observability

Observability in software systems refers to the ability to understand the internal state of a system based on its external outputs, through logging, metrics, and distributed tracing. For AI-powered systems, observability also involves monitoring model outputs, tracking prompt and response quality, and detecting unexpected behavior in production.
