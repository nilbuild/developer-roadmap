# Randomised Algorithms

Randomized algorithms use random numbers as part of their logic, either to avoid worst-case behavior or to approximate solutions. Quicksort with a random pivot is a classic example: randomization makes the worst case very unlikely in practice. Monte Carlo and Las Vegas algorithms are the two main categories.
