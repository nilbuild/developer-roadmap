# Big O Notation

Big-O notation describes the upper bound of an algorithm's growth rate, representing the worst-case scenario. An algorithm that is O(n²) will take at most quadratic time for any input of size n. It is the most commonly used notation in practice because worst-case guarantees matter most for reliability.
