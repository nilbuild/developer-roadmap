# Big-θ Notation

Big-Θ (Theta) notation describes a tight bound, meaning the algorithm grows at exactly that rate in both the best and worst case. If an algorithm is Θ(n log n), its runtime is always proportional to n log n, not just bounded by it. It gives the most precise characterization of an algorithm's complexity.
