# Greedy Algorithms

Greedy algorithms make the locally optimal choice at each step with the hope that this leads to a globally optimal solution. They are fast and simple but do not always produce the best result. Problems where greedy works correctly include activity selection, Huffman encoding, and finding minimum spanning trees.
