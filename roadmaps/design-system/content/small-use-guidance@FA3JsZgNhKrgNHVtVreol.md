# Small Use Guidance

Small use guidance provides specific instructions and design adjustments for rendering a logo at minimal sizes, such as in favicons, mobile headers, or social media avatars. This involves simplifying complex shapes, removing intricate text, or increasing the contrast of visual elements to ensure the brand remains recognizable and legible at very low resolutions. By establishing these constraints, you ensure that the logo maintains its visual integrity and professional appearance across all digital touchpoints.
