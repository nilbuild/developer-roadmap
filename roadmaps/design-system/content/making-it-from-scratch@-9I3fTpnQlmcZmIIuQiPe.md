# From Scratch

There are two general paths for building a design system: creating one from scratch with a blank slate, or building one by analyzing and consolidating an existing product's design. The right approach depends on whether a product already exists and how much design debt has accumulated. Both paths eventually converge on the same outputs: design tokens, documented components, and guidelines.
