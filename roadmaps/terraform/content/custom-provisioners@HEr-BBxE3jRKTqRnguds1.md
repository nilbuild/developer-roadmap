# Custom Provisioners

Custom provisioners can be implemented using external programs that follow the Terraform provisioner plugin protocol. They allow teams to integrate specialized tooling into the Terraform lifecycle without relying on the built-in provisioner types. Custom provisioners are rare and typically only needed for highly specific workflows.
