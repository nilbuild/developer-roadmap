# What and when to use HCP?

HCP Terraform is appropriate when a team wants the benefits of Terraform Cloud, such as remote runs, state management, and policy enforcement, without managing infrastructure for it. It suits teams that are scaling beyond local Terraform workflows and need collaboration features, audit logs, and a managed backend. It is available in free and paid tiers.
