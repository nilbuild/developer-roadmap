# Privacy & Compliance

Cloud compliance means meeting regulatory and legal requirements for how data is stored, processed, and accessed. Common frameworks include GDPR (EU data privacy), HIPAA (US healthcare data), SOC 2 (service organization controls), and PCI DSS (payment card data). Cloud providers offer compliance-ready services and share audit reports, but customers remain responsible for configuring those services correctly.
