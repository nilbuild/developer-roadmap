# VLANs & VXLAN
 
VLANs (Virtual Local Area Networks) segment a physical network into isolated logical networks at Layer 2. In large-scale cloud environments, VLANs alone cannot provide enough isolation across distributed data centers. VXLAN (Virtual Extensible LAN) extends Layer 2 networks over a Layer 3 IP fabric by encapsulating Ethernet frames inside UDP packets. It is the underlying technology that enables VPCs and tenant network isolation to scale across thousands of physical hosts.
