# Cost Monitoring and Budgets
 
Cloud costs are dynamic and can grow unexpectedly as workloads scale or resources are left running. Cost monitoring tools (AWS Cost Explorer, GCP Cost Management, Azure Cost Analysis) provide visibility into spending by service, region, tag, and time period. Budget alerts notify teams when spending approaches or exceeds a defined threshold. Tagging resources consistently is the foundation of cost attribution: without tags, it is difficult to attribute costs to teams, projects, or environments.
