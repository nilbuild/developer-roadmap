# Provider Lock-In
 
Provider lock-in occurs when an application becomes so dependent on a specific cloud provider's proprietary services that migrating to another provider is costly or technically difficult. Deep use of provider-specific services (managed databases, serverless platforms, AI APIs) accelerates lock-in. Strategies to reduce it include using open standards, Kubernetes, and provider-agnostic tools like Terraform, though some degree of lock-in is often an acceptable trade-off for the productivity gains of managed services.
