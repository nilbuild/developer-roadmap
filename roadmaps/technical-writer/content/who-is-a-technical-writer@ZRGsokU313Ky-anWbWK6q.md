# Who is a Technical Writer

A technical writer produces documentation that helps users understand and work with technical products or processes. They work alongside engineers, product managers, and designers to gather information and turn it into structured content like guides, manuals, or API references. The role requires both strong writing ability and enough technical literacy to engage with subject matter experts.
