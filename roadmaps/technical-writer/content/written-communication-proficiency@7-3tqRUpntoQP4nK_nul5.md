# Written Communication Proficiency

Written communication proficiency means producing text that is clear, concise, and appropriate for the intended audience. For technical writers, this includes structuring information logically, maintaining consistent tone, and editing for accuracy. Strong writers know when to simplify and when to preserve technical precision.
