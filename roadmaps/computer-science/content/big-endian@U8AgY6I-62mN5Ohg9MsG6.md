# Big Endian

Big endian is a type of endianness in which the most significant byte is stored at the lowest memory address. This means that the most significant byte is stored first and the least significant byte is stored last. It is the dominant byte order in networking protocols, where it is called network byte order, while most processors, such as x86 and most ARM implementations, are little endian.
