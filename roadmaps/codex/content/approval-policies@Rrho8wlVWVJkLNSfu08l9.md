# Approval Policies

The approval policy controls when Codex stops to ask you. The supported values are `on-request`, where Codex works inside the sandbox and asks before going beyond it, and `never`, where it does not ask. A separate setting, `approvals_reviewer`, decides who answers: you, or an automatic reviewer agent with `auto_review`. That second option is what the "Approve for me" profile uses.
