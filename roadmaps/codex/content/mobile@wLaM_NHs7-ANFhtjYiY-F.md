# Mobile

The ChatGPT mobile app lets you start and follow Codex tasks from your phone. You can run a task in Codex Cloud or control a project on your own computer after pairing it with a QR code. From the app you can send new instructions, approve or deny commands, and review diffs and test results. The docs describe the iOS app, and availability depends on rollout and workspace settings.
