# Models & Usage

Models and usage decide how well Codex performs and how fast you use your allowance. Larger models and higher reasoning effort give better results on hard tasks but use more of your limits. Smaller models are faster and cheaper for simple work. Match the setting to the task, and watch your usage with `/usage` and `/status`.

Visit the following resources to learn more:

- [@official@Models](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/models)
- [@official@Codex pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing)
