# Choosing Models

Choose a model by the kind of task. Use Astra for the hardest reasoning, Sol for long-running work at a lower cost, Terra for visual and computer-use tasks, and Luna for narrow, high-volume jobs. Set it with `/model`, `--model` or `config.toml`. Subagents inherit the parent's model unless you set another one.

Visit the following resources to learn more:

- [@official@Model selection](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/model-selection)
- [@official@Models](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/models)
