# /hooks

`/hooks` shows and manages lifecycle hooks. Hooks that you did not manage yourself must be reviewed and trusted here before they run, and a changed hook needs a new review. You can also disable hooks that are not enforced by an admin.

Visit the following resources to learn more:

- [@official@CLI built-in slash commands](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/developer-commands?surface=cli#cli-built-in-slash-commands)
- [@official@Hooks](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/hooks)
