# Fast Mode

Fast mode is a service tier that returns results sooner at a higher credit cost. It is available where the current model offers it, and in the CLI you toggle it with `/fast`. On eligible plans, Sol also has an Ultrafast tier. Use these modes when you are waiting on the answer, and leave them off for background or overnight work.
