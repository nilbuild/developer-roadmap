# Fast Mode

Fast mode is a service tier that returns results sooner at a higher credit cost, at about 1.5 times the speed of Standard. Ultrafast goes further, at up to 4.5 times Standard speed on GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, on eligible plans. In the CLI you switch with `/fast` or `/ultrafast`, and repeating the active command returns to Standard. Use these modes when you are waiting on the answer, and leave them off for background or overnight work.
