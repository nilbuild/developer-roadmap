# Fast Mode

Fast mode is a service tier that returns results sooner at a higher credit cost. You toggle it with `/fast` when the current model offers it. On eligible plans, Sol also has an Ultrafast tier. Use these modes when you are waiting on the answer, and leave them off for background or overnight work.
