# Permissions & Approvals

Permissions decide what Codex can do without asking you. Two settings work together: the sandbox limits what commands can read, write and reach, and the approval policy decides when Codex must pause for your confirmation. You choose a combination through permission profiles such as Ask for approval, Approve for me and Full access. Start with the most restrictive setting that lets you work.
