# Permissions & Approvals

Permissions decide what Codex can do without asking you. Two settings work together: the sandbox limits what commands can read, write and reach, and the approval policy decides when Codex must pause for your confirmation. In the desktop app and IDE extension you choose a profile such as Ask for approval, Approve for me, Full access or Custom, and in the CLI you change it with `/permissions`. Start with the most restrictive setting that lets you work.
