# Astra

GPT-6.2 Astra (`gpt-6.2-astra`) is the stronger of the two GPT-6.2 models in Codex. It is built for complex work that needs sustained reasoning, such as diagnosing a bug across several services, building a deck from a report, or investigating a research question across sources. Keep it for the tasks that need it and use Sol for everyday work. Astra supports Ultrafast mode on eligible plans.
