# --ask-for-approval

`--ask-for-approval` (or `-a`) sets when Codex pauses for your approval before running a command. The values are `on-request`, where Codex works inside the sandbox and asks before going beyond it, and `never`, where it does not ask. It is the flag form of the `approval_policy` setting.
