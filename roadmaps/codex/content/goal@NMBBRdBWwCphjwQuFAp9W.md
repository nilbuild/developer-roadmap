# /goal

`/goal` sets, edits, pauses, resumes, shows or clears a task goal. The goal text works as both the first prompt and the completion criteria, so Codex keeps working until the outcome is reached. A good goal states the result, the constraints and how to verify it, such as a passing test suite. It keeps the same sandbox and approval settings, and pauses when it needs a decision.
