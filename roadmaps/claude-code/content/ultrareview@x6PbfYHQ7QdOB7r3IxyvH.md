# /ultrareview

`ultrareview` is an alias of `/code-review ultra`. It starts an ultrareview, a deep review that runs as a cloud session on Anthropic's infrastructure. A fleet of reviewer agents explores the change in parallel, and each finding is independently reproduced and verified. It reviews your branch or a pull request, takes roughly 15 to 25 minutes in the background, and needs a [claude.ai](http://claude.ai) login. Pro and Max accounts get three free runs, then it bills as usage credits. `/ultrareview` is an alias when it is available to your account.
