# Open vs Closed Models

Closed models, such as GPT, Claude and Gemini, are used through a provider's API and their weights are not public. Open-weights models, such as Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek and Mistral, can be downloaded, self-hosted and fine-tuned. Closed models usually lead on capability and are simple to start with. Open models give more control over cost, privacy and customization.
