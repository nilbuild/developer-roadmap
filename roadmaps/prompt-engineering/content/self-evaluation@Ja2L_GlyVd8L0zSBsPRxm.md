# Self-Evaluation

Self-evaluation asks a model to review its own output against criteria and then fix problems. A typical pattern is draft, critique, revise. It can catch obvious errors and improve quality, but models are often overconfident about their own mistakes. Tests, sources and other external checks are more reliable when they are available.
