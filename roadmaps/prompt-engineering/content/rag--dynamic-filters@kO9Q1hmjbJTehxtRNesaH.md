# RAG & Dynamic Filters

RAG retrieves relevant documents and adds them to the prompt. Dynamic filters narrow the search with metadata such as date, source, user or document type, so the results are relevant to the request. Good retrieval depends on chunking, embeddings and often a reranking step. Retrieving less but more relevant material usually beats loading everything.
