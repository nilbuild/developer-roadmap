# Reasoning Models

Reasoning models spend extra tokens thinking through a problem before they give a final answer. This makes them stronger at math, logic, coding and multi-step planning, but slower and more expensive than standard models. They need different prompting: clear goals work better than step-by-step instructions, since they already plan their own steps.
