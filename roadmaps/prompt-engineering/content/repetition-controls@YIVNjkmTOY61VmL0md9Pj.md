# Repetition Controls

Repetition controls discourage a model from repeating itself. Frequency penalty lowers the chance of a token the more often it has appeared, while presence penalty lowers the chance of any token that has already appeared. They push the model toward new words and topics. Availability depends on the provider, and they are rarely needed on current models.
