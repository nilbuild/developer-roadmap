# Role Prompting

Role prompting assigns the model a persona or job, such as "You are a senior data analyst." It shapes tone, vocabulary and the perspective of the answer. On current models the gain in accuracy is small, so it is most useful for style and point of view. It is often placed in the system prompt but also works in a user message.
