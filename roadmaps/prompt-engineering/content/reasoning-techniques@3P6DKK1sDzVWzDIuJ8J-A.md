# Reasoning Techniques

Reasoning techniques are prompting methods that help a model work through problems step by step or check its own answers. Some change how the model reasons, such as chain of thought, while others improve reliability by comparing or reviewing several outputs. Reasoning models do much of this internally, so these techniques matter most for standard models.
