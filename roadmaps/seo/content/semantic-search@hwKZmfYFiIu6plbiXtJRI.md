# Semantic Search

Semantic search is a method of finding relevant content based on meaning rather than exact keyword matches. It allows a system to understand that "cheap flights" and "affordable plane tickets" refer to the same idea. This is the foundation that lets modern search and AI systems retrieve relevant content even when the wording differs from the query.
