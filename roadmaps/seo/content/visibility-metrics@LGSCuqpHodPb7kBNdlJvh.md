# Visibility Metrics

Visibility metrics are the main numbers that show how visible a website is in traditional search results. They include impressions (how often the site appears), clicks, click-through rate, and average position. Together they show how much of the available search demand the site captures. Google Search Console reports these numbers in its performance report.
