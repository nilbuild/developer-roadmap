# What is GEO

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization. It is the practice of making content visible inside AI-generated answers, such as those produced by ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, or Perplexity. Unlike SEO, which targets a list of ranked links, GEO targets the retrieval systems that AI models pull from when generating a response.
