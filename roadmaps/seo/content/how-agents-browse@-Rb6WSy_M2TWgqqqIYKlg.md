# How Agents Browse

How agents browse describes the way an AI agent moves through websites on its own to finish a task. The agent loads a page, reads its text and structure, follows links, and works with forms and buttons, usually without a person guiding each step. Clear HTML, labeled elements, and simple navigation make a site easier for agents to understand and use.
