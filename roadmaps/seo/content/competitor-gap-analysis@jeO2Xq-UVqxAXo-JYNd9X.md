# Competitor Gap Analysis

Competitor gap analysis is the process of identifying the topics, keywords, and content formats that your competitors are ranking for but your website is not. By comparing your existing content against the top-performing pages in your industry, you can pinpoint specific areas where your site is missing information or failing to address user intent. This practice helps you discover untapped opportunities to create more comprehensive resources and capture traffic that currently flows to other sites.
