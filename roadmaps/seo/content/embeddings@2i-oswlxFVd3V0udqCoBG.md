# Embeddings

Embeddings are numerical representations of text that capture its meaning, allowing a computer to compare how similar two pieces of text are. Words or sentences with related meanings end up close together in this numerical space, even if they share no common words. Search and AI systems use embeddings to power semantic and vector search.
