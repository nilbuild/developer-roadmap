# Entity Consistency

Entity consistency is how uniformly a brand or person's identity appears across the web, including directories, social profiles, and mentions that are not directly controlled by that brand. Search and AI systems use this consistency to confirm that scattered mentions across the internet all refer to the same real entity, rather than several unrelated ones.
