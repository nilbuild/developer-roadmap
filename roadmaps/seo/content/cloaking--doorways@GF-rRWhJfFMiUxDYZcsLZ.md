# Cloaking & Doorways

Cloaking is showing search engines different content than what a real visitor sees, typically to manipulate rankings while displaying something else to users. Doorway pages are low-value pages created specifically to rank for narrow search terms and funnel visitors elsewhere, rather than serving as genuine destinations. Both are explicitly against search engine guidelines.
