# Nofollow, Noindex, and Disallow

Nofollow, noindex, and disallow are instructions used to manage how search engines interact with your website. A "nofollow" tag tells search engines not to pass authority to a specific link, a "noindex" meta tag prevents a page from appearing in search results, and a "disallow" command in a robots.txt file instructs search engine crawlers to avoid scanning certain parts of your site entirely. These tools give you control over which pages are indexed and how crawling resources are allocated across your domain.
