# RAG

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It is a method where an AI system first retrieves relevant documents or passages from an external source, then uses that retrieved content to generate its answer, rather than relying only on what it learned during training. This is the mechanism behind how many AI systems produce answers that cite or reflect current web content.
