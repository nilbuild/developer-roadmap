# Niche Forums

Niche forums are small online communities built around one industry, hobby, or interest. Members share detailed and honest opinions, questions, and experiences that general platforms rarely cover. Search engines and AI systems often use these discussions to answer specific questions in that niche, so taking part in them can build a brand's visibility and reputation.
