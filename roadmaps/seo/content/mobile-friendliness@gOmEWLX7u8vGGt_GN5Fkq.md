# Mobile Friendliness

Mobile friendliness is whether a website works properly on a phone screen, including readable text, tappable buttons, and no horizontal scrolling. Most search engines evaluate a site primarily by its mobile version rather than its desktop version, since most searches happen on mobile devices.
