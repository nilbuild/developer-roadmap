# Site Hierarchy & Depth

Site hierarchy is the structure of how pages relate to each other, from the homepage down through categories to individual pages. Depth refers to how many clicks it takes to reach a given page from the homepage. A flatter hierarchy, where important pages are just a few clicks away, is generally easier for crawlers to find and fully explore.
