# Rank Tracking

Rank tracking is the practice of monitoring the position of a website in search results for chosen keywords over time. Dedicated tools check the search results on a regular schedule and record each change. The data shows which keywords are gaining or losing positions, and it helps measure the effect of SEO changes. Many trackers also let you filter results by location, device, and search engine.
