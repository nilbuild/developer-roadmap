# Dense Retrieval

Dense retrieval is a method of finding relevant documents using embeddings and vector similarity, rather than matching exact keywords. It compares the numerical representation of a query against the numerical representations of stored content to find the closest matches by meaning. It is the retrieval method most closely associated with semantic and vector search.
