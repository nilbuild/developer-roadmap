# Spam Policies

Spam policies are the specific rules search engines publish defining manipulative practices that violate their guidelines, such as link schemes, cloaking, or abusing expired domains. Violating them can result in a manual action or an automatic ranking penalty, so understanding the specific categories helps practitioners avoid crossing lines unintentionally.
