# Preferred Sources

Preferred sources are the specific websites and publications that a given AI system consistently favors when choosing what to cite, often shaped by factors like established authority, structured content, or crawl accessibility. Identifying which sources an AI system prefers within a given topic helps inform where content or PR efforts might be most effective.
