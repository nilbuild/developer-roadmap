# Post-Migration Monitoring

Post-migration monitoring is the tracking of a website's rankings, traffic, indexing, and crawl behavior after a migration is complete. The goal is to find problems early, such as broken redirects, missing pages, crawl errors, or sudden drops in traffic. Teams compare the new data with the numbers from before the move and fix issues quickly. This work usually continues for several weeks or months until performance becomes stable.
