# SERP, Index, Crawl

A SERP, or Search Engine Results Page, is the page shown after someone runs a search. The index is the search engine's stored database of pages it has processed and can retrieve. A crawl is the act of a bot visiting and reading a page's content, which happens before that page can be added to the index.
