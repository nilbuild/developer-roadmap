# Entity SEO

Entity SEO is the practice of optimizing for how search and AI systems understand specific, distinct real-world things, such as a person, company, or product, rather than just matching strings of text. It focuses on making sure a system can correctly identify what an entity is, how it relates to other entities, and whether different mentions across the web refer to the same one.
