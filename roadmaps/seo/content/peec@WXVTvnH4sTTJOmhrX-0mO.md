# Peec AI

Peec is a platform that tracks a brand's presence in AI-generated answers. It runs a defined set of prompts and reports visibility, sentiment, and share of voice. Users can compare their results with competitors and see which sources the AI systems cite. This helps teams decide where to improve their content and brand mentions.
