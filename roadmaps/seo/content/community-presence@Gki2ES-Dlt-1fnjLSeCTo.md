# Community Presence

Community presence is a brand or individual's activity and reputation within online communities such as Reddit, Quora, and niche forums, where people ask questions and share opinions candidly. These communities are increasingly pulled from directly by AI systems when forming answers, making genuine presence there valuable beyond traditional SEO value.
