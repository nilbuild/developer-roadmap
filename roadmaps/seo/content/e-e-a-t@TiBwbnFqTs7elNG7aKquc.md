# E-E-A-T

E-E-A-T stands for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness. It is a framework Google uses to judge the quality and credibility of content and its creator, rather than a direct, measurable ranking factor. AI systems increasingly use similar signals when deciding which sources to trust and cite.
