# Introduction

Search is no longer just about ranking in a list of blue links. People now find answers through Google's AI Overviews, chat assistants like ChatGPT and Claude, and voice-based answer engines, alongside traditional search results. This roadmap covers SEO, GEO, and AEO as three connected layers of the same discipline: getting found, getting cited, and getting your content pulled into a direct answer. It walks through technical foundations, content strategy, entity and authority signals, and measurement, so you can build visibility across both classic search engines and the AI systems now sitting alongside them.
