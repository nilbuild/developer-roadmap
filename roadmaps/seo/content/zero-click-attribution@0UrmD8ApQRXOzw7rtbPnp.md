# Zero-Click Attribution

Zero-click attribution is the practice of measuring the value of searches where a user gets their answer directly on the results page, or from an AI-generated response, without ever clicking through to a website. These searches used to be largely invisible to standard analytics, even though they still represent real brand exposure.
