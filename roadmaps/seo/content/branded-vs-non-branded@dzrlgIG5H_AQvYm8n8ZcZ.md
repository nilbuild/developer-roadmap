# Branded vs Non-Branded

Branded queries are searches that contain a company's name, such as "Nike running shoes". Non-branded queries are generic searches without the name, such as "best running shoes". Comparing the two shows if traffic comes mostly from people who already know the brand or from new people who find it through general topics.
