# Local Search

Local search covers the baseline setup required to appear in local search results, before rankings can meaningfully improve. This includes claiming and completing a Google Business Profile, keeping business details consistent everywhere they appear, gathering citations from other sites, and building dedicated pages for each location or service area served.
