# Attributes & Relationships

Attributes are facts a system tracks about a specific entity, such as a company's founding year or industry. Relationships describe how entities connect to each other, such as a person founding a particular company. Together they let a system answer questions that require connecting information across multiple sources, not just retrieving one page.
