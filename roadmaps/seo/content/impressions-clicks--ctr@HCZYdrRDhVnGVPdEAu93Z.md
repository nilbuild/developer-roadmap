# Impressions, Clicks & CTR

Impressions are how often a page appears in search results for a query, whether or not anyone clicks it. Clicks are how many people actually click through. CTR, or click-through rate, is the percentage of impressions that turn into clicks, and a low CTR relative to ranking position can signal that a title or description is not compelling enough.
