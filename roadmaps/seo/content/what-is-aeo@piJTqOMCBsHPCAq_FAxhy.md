# What is AEO

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It focuses on structuring content so answer systems, such as featured snippets, voice assistants, and chat interfaces, can extract a direct answer from it. The goal is making information easy to pull out and present cleanly, rather than just easy to find.
