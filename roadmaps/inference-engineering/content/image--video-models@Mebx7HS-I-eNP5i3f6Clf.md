# Image & Video Models

Image and video models are specialized artificial intelligence architectures designed to process, interpret, and generate visual data. These models utilize techniques like convolutional neural networks (CNNs), transformers, and diffusion processes to analyze pixel patterns for tasks such as object detection, image classification, and video frame synthesis.
