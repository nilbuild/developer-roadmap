# Blackwell

The Blackwell architecture, released in late 2024 with the B200 and B300, added FP4 Tensor Cores and new microscaling number formats including MXFP8, MXFP4, and NVFP4. B200 GPUs offer approximately 5 petaFLOPS of FP8 compute and up to 8 TB/s of memory bandwidth. They are the current gold standard for demanding inference workloads including large LLMs and video generation.
