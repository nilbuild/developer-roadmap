# vLLM

vLLM is the most widely adopted LLM inference engine, offering broad support for model architectures, hardware platforms (NVIDIA, AMD, Google TPUs), and optimization techniques including prefix caching, quantization, speculative decoding, and parallelism. Its primary advantage is ease of adoption and the fastest day-zero support for newly released open models. It also supports multimodal inference via vLLM Omni.
