# Multi-Cloud

Multi-cloud inference distributes GPU capacity across providers and regions to maximize availability, minimize latency, and meet data residency requirements. This group covers procurement strategy, capacity management, geographic routing, and the reliability patterns that make large-scale inference resilient to hardware and provider failures.
