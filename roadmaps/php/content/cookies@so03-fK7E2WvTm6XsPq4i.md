# Cookies

Cookies are small key-value strings stored in the user's browser and sent with every request to the matching domain. PHP sets cookies with `setcookie()` and reads them through `$_COOKIE`. Cookies can have expiration times, domain restrictions, and security flags like `Secure` and `HttpOnly` to limit exposure.

Visit the following resources to learn more:

- [@official@Cookies](https://www.php.net/manual/en/features.cookies.php)
